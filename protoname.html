What is our ID Card and why is it useful?

This ID Card is a digital lanyard that we have created for neurodivergent people. It allows others to become aware of a neurodivergent trait you may possess, that makes it more difficult for you to navigate through public areas.

Imagine you are at an Emergency Room in a hospital. There is a lot of noise, and it is incredibly bright. As a result, you are struggling to concentrate, and the doctors cannot communicate with you efficiently. To avoid this outcome, you can show others this app that informs them of, in this case, your sensitivity to light and noise, and so they can make the area dimmer and reduce noise to help you recompose yourself.

Note: This is a prototype website and would not be utilised as an app until the concept has been approved at a later date.

How does it work?

The ID Card consists of flash cards, where you can display information about your neurodivergent trait.

For example, you can specify that you are non-verbal on a flash card, and provide extra information about what people should do if they are attempting to communicate with you.

For more information on flash cards, navigate to Extra Help at the top of this page.
