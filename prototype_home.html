ID Card Home Page

Welcome to the home page for the Digital ID Card. Here, you can make a digital lanyard that lets people know you may be neurodivergent and require patience or help in public areas.

Note: This is a prototype website, and is subject to change.

If you have a hidden disability, click here to set up an ID Card.

Flash Cards

You can also set up a flash card. This will tell people what to do in specific situations.

For instance, you may become non-verbal depending on your mood, and so you can explain how people should react to this on your flash card.

You may make up to 5 flash cards.
