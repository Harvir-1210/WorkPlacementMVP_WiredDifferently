What is our ID Card and why is it useful?

This ID Card is a digital lanyard that we created for neurodivergent people, allowing you to express to others if you need help in public areas due to a specific trait.

● Imagine you are at an Emergency Room in a hospital. There is lots of noise and light that overwhelms you. With our ID Card, you can signal the doctors to reduce the noise and make the room dimmer to help you recompose yourself.

Note: This is a prototype website and would not be utilised as an app until the concept has been approved at a later date.

How does it work?

The ID Card consists of flash cards, where you can display information about your neurodivergent trait.

● For example, you can specify that you are non-verbal on a flash card, and provide extra information about what people should do to support you.

For more information on flash cards, navigate to Extra Help at the top of this page.
